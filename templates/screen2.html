% Speculative & Multi-Token Inference Synthesis
\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

Leviathan \etal~\cite{leviathan2023fast} formalized speculative decoding using an auxiliary draft model with rejection sampling. While lossless, this approach incurs substantial VRAM overhead. Medusa~\cite{cai2024medusa} mitigates this via multiple prediction heads atop the primary backbone, though relaxing exact sampling. Meanwhile, SpecAdapt~\cite{zhang2024specadapt} and SpecInfer~\cite{miao2023specinfer} demonstrate that structured tree verification scales acceptance rates up to 3.4x while maintaining exact distribution guarantees.
